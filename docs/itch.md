## Description

**Both fighters pick a move in secret. Then both happen at once.**

USC, the Ultimate Stickfight Championship, is a turn based MMA game about reading your
opponent. Strikes beat
takedowns, takedowns beat blocks, blocks beat strikes, but every rule has a twist:
a jab is too quick to block a power punch, a takedown walks through a leg kick, and a
feint freezes someone who is waiting to counter.

- **Stand up, clinch and ground.** Guard, mount and back control each have their own
  moves. Submissions are a guessing game: pick the side they will not defend.
- **Stamina, damage and momentum.** Body shots drain the tank, leg kicks take away
  power, cuts bring in the doctor. Big moments get the crowd behind you. Fill the meter
  and your next attack goes all in.
- **Fight cards.** Superman Punch, Showtime Kick, Imanari Roll, Peruvian Necktie, Twister
  and more. Build a deck that suits your style.
- **Career.** Start at the bottom, pick up a rival, go to camp before title fights, win
  the belt, defend it and retire into the hall of fame.
- **Tournaments.** Eight fighters, one night. The damage carries over.
- **Weight classes**, from lightweight to heavyweight, and catchweight bouts.
- **Daily challenge, achievements and secret fighters** to unlock.
- **Two players on one keyboard or with controllers.** Picks stay hidden.
- A tutorial gets you fighting in two minutes.

Free and open source: https://github.com/Vavoss123/stickfight

## Install notes

- **Windows:** run Stickfight.exe. If Windows SmartScreen warns you, click More info
  and Run anyway (the game is not code signed).
- **macOS:** unzip, then right-click Stickfight and choose Open the first time.
- **Linux:** unpack and run ./Stickfight.
